Cookie Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 | Amber Garb Quest

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small alphanumeric text files stored on your computer, tablet, or mobile device by websites you visit. They enable sites to remember user preferences, maintain session state, and provide aggregated analytics that help webmasters improve user experience.

2. Categories of Cookies We Utilize

  • Essential & Technical Cookies: Strictly necessary for core navigation, mobile menu rendering, and form security. The site cannot function properly without them.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: We utilize Google Analytics (via tag ID G-0LY0HY7L01) to collect anonymized data regarding visitor volume, session duration, and popular pages. This data contains no personally identifiable information.
  • Preference Cookies: Enable our website to remember your preferred styling matrix configurations and font display settings.

3. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You can configure your browser settings to accept, reject, or alert you whenever a cookie is transmitted. Most browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) allow you to delete stored cookies through their Privacy and Security preferences.
