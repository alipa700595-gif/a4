Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 | Amber Garb Quest

1. Introduction and Commitment to Privacy

Amber Garb Quest ("we," "our," or "the Atelier") operates the website ambergarbquest.com. We are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and data autonomy of our patrons and website visitors. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and safeguard personal information in full compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and relevant international privacy standards.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide seamless styling concierge services, process bespoke inquiries, and improve your digital experience. This information includes:

  • Directly Provided Information: Full name, email address, telephone number, physical delivery address, sizing measurements, and specific styling notes submitted via our inquiry forms.
  • Automated Device & Browsing Data: Anonymized IP address, browser type, operating system version, referring URLs, pages visited, and interaction timestamps collected via cookies and analytical tags.

3. Lawful Basis and Purposes of Data Processing

We process your personal information under the following legitimate legal bases:

  • Contractual Fulfillment: To fulfill concierge consultations, dispatch textile swatches, and respond to your inquiries.
  • Legitimate Business Interests: To optimize our website layout, secure our network infrastructure against fraudulent behavior, and analyze broad demographic trends.
  • User Consent: To transmit our bi-monthly Amber Garb Gazette newsletter when explicitly subscribed by you.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers. We share minimal necessary data strictly with trusted service providers who assist our operations, such as secure web hosting providers, analytics platforms (Google Analytics 4), and communication delivery systems. All third-party processors are legally bound to uphold stringent data protection standards.

5. Data Retention and Security Measures

We retain personal information only for as long as required to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with statutory legal and accounting obligations. We implement industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption, secure server architecture, and restricted administrative access protocols to protect your data against unauthorized access or disclosure.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have specific legal rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access to obtain a copy of the data we hold concerning you.
  • Right to Rectification to correct inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") to request deletion of your information.
  • Right to Object or Restrict processing under certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability in a structured, machine-readable format.

7. Contact Information for Data Privacy Inquiries
